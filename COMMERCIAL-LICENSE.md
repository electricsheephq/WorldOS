# WorldOS Commercial License Summary

This is a plain-English summary of `LICENSE` and `ROYALTY-ADDENDUM.md`. If this
summary conflicts with those files, the legal terms in `LICENSE` and
`ROYALTY-ADDENDUM.md` control.

License type: custom source-available commercial EULA, not OSI open source.

WorldOS is source-available. The source is available for inspection, learning,
modification, non-commercial use, and commercial use under the tiers below.

## Best One-Liner

Indies pay $20 to ship and only share revenue after they win. Studios need a
commercial license from day one.

art, audio, generated output, and other original work. ElectricSheep owns
WorldOS Technology, trademarks, and ElectricSheep IP. Third-party materials keep
their own licenses.

## Non-Commercial Use

Free for personal, educational, evaluation, research, hobby, home tabletop, and
internal learning use that is not connected to revenue, funding, paid services,
paid distribution, publisher-backed development, monetization, or business
operations.

## Non-Royalty Uses

No royalty is owed for these uses by themselves:

- private internal evaluation, research, and learning;
- private home tabletop campaigns that are not monetized;
- static exports, screenshots, pitch images, world bibles, campaign notes, and
  other generated output used privately or internally and not sold, licensed,
  monetized, publisher-backed, or included in a commercial Project; and
- indirect revenue not tied to Project access, Project features, in-Project
  benefits, or a commercial release shaped by WorldOS.

## Indie Commercial License

You qualify as an indie only if all of these are true:

- annual gross revenue is under USD $1,000,000;
- total funding is under USD $1,000,000;
- the Project is not publisher-backed, publisher-funded, or publisher-directed;
  and
- 10 or fewer employees and contractors work on the Project.

Indie commercial terms, if You qualify and pay the Project fee when Commercial
Use begins:

- USD $20 one-time per commercial Project;
- 0% royalty on the first USD $1,000,000 of lifetime gross Project revenue; and
- 2.5% royalty only on royalty revenue remaining after that lifetime exclusion.

## Studio Commercial License

Studio terms apply from day one if any of these are true:

- annual gross revenue is over USD $1,000,000;
- total funding is over USD $1,000,000;
- development is publisher-backed, publisher-funded, or publisher-directed; or
- more than 10 employees and contractors work on the Project.

Studio commercial terms:

- 2.5% royalty if WorldOS is used for design, universe generation, tabletop
  campaign tooling, prototyping, pre-production, export, narrative/world bible,
  static output, or content pipeline work and WorldOS Runtime Code does not
  ship; or
- 5% royalty if WorldOS Runtime Code, the playable tabletop/game shell, agentic
  world simulation, generated-play loop, or a substantially complete
  WorldOS-derived playable experience ships to users or players.

If both categories apply, the 5% runtime rate applies.

## Royalty Examples

- Indie game earns USD $1,200,000 lifetime royalty revenue: first USD
  $1,000,000 is royalty-free, then 2.5% applies to USD $200,000.
- Studio uses WorldOS to generate a world bible for a game built in another
  engine and no WorldOS Runtime Code ships: 2.5% applies.
- Studio ships a WorldOS-derived playable shell or agentic simulation: 5%
  applies.
- Private, non-monetized home tabletop play: no royalty.

## Reporting

Royalties are calculated quarterly after a royalty obligation is reached.
Reports and payments are due within 45 days after each calendar quarter in which
royalties are owed. See `ROYALTY-ADDENDUM.md` for included revenue, exclusions,
records, audit, tax, and late-payment details.
