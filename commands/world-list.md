The player wants to see the available world seeds.

Do this:
1. Call `worldos-engine` `list_worlds`.
2. Present each world: its **name**, a one-line premise, the **era** (when it's set), and the tone. Mention `lore_pages` — how much canon the DM can pull on demand via `lookup_lore`.
3. Invite the player to step into one with `/world-play <id>` (e.g. `/world-play sundered-reach`), or author a new one with `/world-new`.

Keep it short and inviting — a menu of worlds to step into.
