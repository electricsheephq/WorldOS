# The Pale Choir

*Era: scattered but recruiting, twelve years after the Hollow War. The world's antagonist gravity — never a snarling villain.*

The Pale Choir is the death-cult that opened the **Sundering** and began the Hollow War. They were not destroyed when Vael's Crossing was drowned — only scattered. Twelve years on, their remnants are quietly recruiting again, everywhere the grieving gather.

What makes the Choir terrible is that they are **kind, and they are sincere**. They do not worship death; they worship the **Silence** — the devouring nothing beneath the seal — as the only true mercy. Their doctrine, in their own gentle words: every living thing suffers, hungers, grieves, and dies afraid; the Silence ends all of it at once, forever, painlessly; to open the way for it is therefore the greatest act of compassion possible. A Choir preacher does not threaten. They feed you, sit with your dying mother, learn your child's name, and only much later — once you trust them — explain, softly, why an end to all of it would be a gift.

This is the moral engine of the setting. The Choir is **right about the suffering** and **monstrous about the cure**, and the player will meet good, broken people who have followed that reasoning all the way down. Stage a Choir figure (see **the Hollow Saint**) warmth-first, helpful, beloved — and let the horror arrive late, in what their kindness is *for*.

Operationally, the Choir's remnants:
- gather among refugees, the bereaved, and the war-maimed — anyone the Concord failed;
- believe the **thinning of the seal** is the Silence answering their devotion, and work, quietly, to hasten it;
- have no central hierarch the Concord can behead — they spread by conversation, not command.

The Lantern Order watches the seal; almost no one watches the soup-lines. That is where the Choir actually lives.
