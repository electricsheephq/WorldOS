# Vael's Crossing (the Drowned City) & the Seal

*Era: a ruin twelve years on; the seal beneath it is rumored to be thinning. The war's open wound.*

Vael's Crossing was a great river-city until the **Pale Choir** opened the **Sundering** beneath it and the **Silence** began to unmake the Reach. The only working that could close the rift had to be set from beneath the water — so the Ashen Concord, on the order of the **Vael line** that ruled the city, **drowned Vael's Crossing whole** to set the seal. The sea took the spires, the rift, and thousands of souls together. (See *The Hollow War*.)

Now it is a tidal ruin on the grey coast. At high water there is only open sea and the tips of the tallest towers; at **low tide** the drowned city surfaces — streets of black silt, doorways still hung with rotted cloth, the great civic bell green with weed — and you can walk down into it for a few cold hours before the water returns. People who linger past the turn of the tide are not always recovered.

**The seal** lies at the city's heart, where the rift was. The **Lantern Order** keeps a watch-post on the high ground above the shallows and sends wardens down at every low tide to read the seal and bring up the drowned for burial. What they will not say aloud: the seal is **thinning**. Lanterns gutter without wind near it. The water there is a few degrees too cold. Sounds carry wrong — a held note under everything, like the city is still ringing its bell from below. The child-oracle **Lys** dreams it; **Old Tam** knows a back way down and what the Order pretends isn't there.

For the player, Vael's Crossing is the setting's keystone location: the proof of what the war cost, the place the slow main thread (the thinning seal) lives, and the most dangerous ground in the Reach. Treat any descent into it as a felt threat — show the Silence's edge *before* anyone decides to press deeper.
