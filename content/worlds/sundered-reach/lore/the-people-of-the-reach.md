# The People of the Reach (the figures who matter)

*Era: twelve years after the Hollow War. Pull them in as allies, patrons, complications, or a companion; they have their own lives — invent freely on top.*

- **Maren Vael** — last of the Vael line that gave the order to **drown its own city**, and reluctant Lord-Warden of Brassmoor. A weary, decent, compromised war-hero who carries that guilt like a stone and leads only because no one else will. Speaks plainly, trusts slowly, would burn her own name to spare the Reach a second drowning. **She knows the seal is thinning and is hiding it from the Concord** to prevent a panic that would tip into civil war.

- **The Hollow Saint** — a Pale Choir hierophant, and the world's warmth-first antagonist. Soft-spoken, genuinely gentle, beloved by the refugees she feeds and tends. She believes the **Silence** is the only true mercy, and every kind word she speaks is sincere — that is what makes her monstrous. Stage her kindness long before her horror; let the player like her, and let her make her case in her own voice. (See *The Pale Choir*.)

- **Castellan Bram Oye** — a Lantern Order knight and the player's likeliest **companion**. Grizzled, dry, unshakeably steady; hands you a waterskin before a fight and means it. He held the line at the Drowning and **let a sworn friend go under to set the seal** — a wound he never speaks of and never stops carrying. He argues for mercy toward the Choir's pawns and is iron about the Silence itself. His grief activates whenever the seal or the Drowning is touched. (Recruit him with `recruit_companion`.)

- **Lys** — a quiet, watchful refugee child of the drowned lands who **dreams true things** and is frightened of them. Trusts almost no one, says little, and what she says matters. She dreamed the seal's thinning before any warden would credit it — the first warning, dismissed because of who carried it.

- **Vance Greywater** — merchant-prince and spymaster of the Greywater League. Charming, precise, ruthless; a smile like a contract. The fastest way to learn anything in the Reach — at a price that is never only coin. Loves the Reach the way a man loves a thing he intends to own.

- **Old Tam** — a Brassmoor fixture nursing watered ale and old war-medals. Bitter, funny, and a sharper witness than anyone credits: he buried a great many of the Reach's dead and remembers where. He knows a **back way into the drowned city**, and what the Lantern Order pretends isn't down there.
