# Brassmoor

*The Concord's seat, twelve years after the Hollow War.*

Brassmoor is the proud, corrupt capital of the Sundered Reach — a tiered port city of brass-capped towers and refugee-choked under-streets, the closest thing the free cities have to a center. It is the seat of the **Ashen Concord**, the fraying wartime council, and its harbor still flies the banners of a dozen cities that no longer trust one another.

The upper tiers belong to the merchant-houses and the Concord's halls; the lower lanes flood with those who fled the drowning of Vael's Crossing and the grey moors of the Hollowmarch. Ash gathers in the gutters here too, though lighter than at the coast. The **Lantern Order** keeps a chapter-house near the harbor; the **Greywater League** keeps counting-houses everywhere.

Brassmoor is the natural hub: the party resupplies, hears rumors, and is pulled into the Reach's threads here — the contested leadership of the Concord, the quiet recruiting of the Pale Choir, and the first whispers that the seal beneath the waves is thinning.
