# Baldur's Gate

*Era: 1492 DR, the winter after the fall of the Absolute.*

Baldur's Gate is the great mercantile city-state of the Western Heartlands, straddling the river Chionthar near its mouth on the Sea of Swords. Founded on the legend of the explorer **Balduran**, it grew rich and ruthless on trade. It is ruled by the **Council of Four** (the dukes) and the **Parliament of Peers** — the *patriars*, its entrenched noble families — and policed by the mercenary **Flaming Fist**.

The city has three parts. The **Upper City**, behind the Basilisk Gate, holds the patriars' marble estates, the High Hall, the Counting House, and the fortress of **Wyrm's Rock**. The **Lower City**, inside the old walls and down to the harbor, is the crowded heart — the Elfsong Tavern, Sorcerous Sundries, Baldur's Mouth gazette, dockside dens, and the temples. The **Outer City** sprawls beyond the walls: Rivington, smugglers' docks, and the desperate who cannot afford to live inside.

**Now:** the city is traumatized and rebuilding. The Cult of the Absolute's Netherbrain breached the Lower City and was destroyed in the harbor; the scar remains. Archduke **Enver Gortash** is dead, his **Steel Watch** wrecked, and the seat of power is contested. Thousands who carried Absolute tadpoles are feared and shunned. Power is up for grabs, and not every cultist, devil, or freed mind flayer was accounted for.
