# Gond Gate

The **Gond Gate** was a gate and local landmark in the city of Baldur's Gate. It was located in the region of the Old Wall that separated the Temples District in the Upper City from the Steeps in the Lower City.

The Gond Gate was one of the city's "patriar gates", four well-controlled entrances that connected the Lower City and Upper City regions. These gates were typically only accessible to select individuals, such as the city's wealthy patriar, those persons bearing their livery, or certain criminal Kingpins.
