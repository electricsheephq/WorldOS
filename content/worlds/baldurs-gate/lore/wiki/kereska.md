# Kereska

**Kereska** () was the chaotic neutral dragon goddess of magic, specifically dragon magic and magical creativity.

## Description
Kereska always manifested in the form of a dragon, although she was able to take on many draconic forms as she wanted to, and specific details about her natural form were unknown. However, she was always surrounded by a pulsating aura of magical power.

## Realm
It was believed Kereska had her lair in the plane of Limbo.

## Abilities
Kereska was reputed to have the ability to cast spells as many times per day as she wanted to, and as she was the font of magical creativity, she was able to create any new spell-like power with a moment's effort.

She also had the ability to empower any draconic creature with whatever level of spellcasting ability she wished, and often bestowed her followers with the ability to cast new spells and spell-like abilities if they had pleased her.

Kereska was noted to grant magic to many chromatic dragons, including their breath weapons.

## Worshipers
Kereska was revered by dragons of all kinds, although those adept with magic were specially devoted to her. Dragons researching new spells often invoked her name in hopes she would imbue them with her power. Kereska's divine aims were so aligned with those of Mystra that some followers were able to claim patronage from both.

## History
According to draconic myths, it was Kereska who first taught dragonkind how to wield magic.

Over the centuries leading up to the Time of Troubles, Kereska's supremacy as the dragon goddess of magic was increasingly challenged by the growing influence of the demigod Kalzareinad. Ever since aiding Sammaster in creating the first Cult dracolich in 902 DR, Kalzareinad had actively assisted mortals and dragons in developing innovative, often reckless magic. This rapid expansion of his domain brought the two draconic powers into direct competition.

During this era of growing rivalry, sometime prior to 1357 DR, Kereska empowered the gold dragon sorcerer Larendrammagar as a reward for his magical achievements. She granted him the ability to cast more spells than any dragon naturally could, and to wield both arcane and divine magic.

The rivalry between the two deities finally reached its climax in the . Kalzareinad unwittingly utilized "casting strings", or spell elements, created by Kereska. This fatal misstep inextricably linked him to the goddess, placing him under her absolute control. Kereska swiftly absorbed his power, subsuming his divine portfolio and worshipers entirely, before casting his corpse adrift upon the Astral Plane.

With her dominion over draconic magic secured, Kereska's influence gradually extended beyond dragonkind. By 1492 DR, she had imbued the staff *Markoheshkir* with her power, granting a humanoid wielder elemental energy sourced from the goddess.
