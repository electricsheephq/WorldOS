# Admiral's Way

**Admiral's Way** was a dockside street in the Lower City of Baldur's Gate.

## Description
Admiral's Way was located in the Eastway, a single city block south of Sailor's Crossing plaza. The Low Lantern festhall was docked at the eastern end of Admiral's Way in the 15 century DR.

## History
In the summer of the , on Kythorn 19, the Sailor's Crossing plaza became a scene of a grizzly murder, located just within earshot of Admiral's Way. A puking human male was reported being on Admiral's Way shortly after the murder took place, but the detective missed the opportunity to interrogate the likely witness.
