# Lost library of Lethchauntos (Baldur's Gate)

The so-called **lost library of Lethchauntos** in Baldur's Gate was one of five hidden libraries across Faerûn built by the orc wizard Lethchauntos sometime in or after the 10 century DR.

## Location
The library was hidden in the city sewers.

## History
After his home in Phandalin was overrun by the orcs of Uruth Ukrypt during the , Lethchauntos toured the Realms to build his libraries and bury the deceased members of his family at each one. The Baldur's Gate library was the last of the five libraries he built.

In the , after finding records about Lethchauntos while in Phandalin, two aspiring mages named Myrna Nor and Powell Niccam deduced the locations of Lethchauntos's five libraries.

## Inhabitants
Within the library, Lethchauntos left behind an imp named Bitterbite imprisoned in a glass sphere.

By the turn of the 16 century DR, an indifferent ghoul named Slymon had made his lair in the library.
