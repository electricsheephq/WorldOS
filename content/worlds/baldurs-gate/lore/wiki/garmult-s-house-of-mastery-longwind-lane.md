# Garmult's House of Mastery (Longwind Lane)

**Garmult's House of Mastery** was an academy founded by retired sea captain and fleet owner Darbrand Garmult on Longwind Lane in Baldur's Gate active in the 14 century DR. The center of learning officially taught law and commercial business. In fact, Garmult used his establishment to direct his pupils to the financing of piracy and other illegal endeavors, as he was himself a smuggler.
