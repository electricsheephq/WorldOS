# Hulkael Street

**Hulkael Street** was a short street in the Heapside district of Baldur's Gate, near the city's eastern walls.

## Location
Hulkael Street was a much larger street that cut across Heapside, Eastway, and Brampton towards the Fox Bottom moot; however, the street became dramatically shortened by the 15 century DR. Hulkael Street began at the strake called Lathdell, near the Shrine of the Suffering, and moved to the Murl's Rest strake where Sorcerous Sundries stood.
