# Nashkel

**Nashkel** was a fortified Amnian town on the northern slopes of the Cloud Peaks in west Faerûn. Although a part of Amn, Nashkel was separated from the rest of the realm by the mountains and was relatively autonomous. Thanks to its mountainous locale, it was also called the **Cold City**, the **Windy City**, and the **Sloping City of Amn**.

## Geography
The town lay on the lower northern slopes of the Cloud Peaks and, at an altitude of  above sea level, it was the highest town in Amn, with the next highest being Imnescar and those around Lake Weng. Its outlying farms, which actually extended into the Green Fields, marked the northernmost extent of Amn's claimed territory, with the town lying outside the border proper It was also the coldest place in Amn. It suffered harsh winter storms every year and blizzards in the high mountain passes could leave Nashkel cut off from greater Amn for months at a time.

The Trade Way ran through here, from Beregost to Crimmor. The stretch south to Crimmor was known locally as the Bitten Road as it passed between the twin peaks called the Fangs.

## Government
The town was led by a mayor; *circa* 1370 DR, this was the retired heroic adventurer Berrun Ghastkill. The Ghastkills were a leading family of Nashkel.

## Trade
The townsfolk cultivated barley, from which they brewed beer; raised sheep and harvested their wool; and hunted game for the meat and pelts. There was little demand for furs in Amn proper, owing to the warm clime, but its merchants were happy to trade it onwards. In particular, the Nashkel Store paid well for winter wolf pelts. There was also a productive iron mine in the mountains above Nashkel.

But primarily Nashkel was a key stop along the main overland trade route between Amn and the Sword Coast, but was frequently isolated from the south by winter blizzards and storms in the mountain passes. Caravans and travelers from the north stopped here before continuing south through the mountains. The Nashkars provided expert guides for those traveling in the nearby mountains, as well as supplies, equipment, and information for caravans as they passed through. At least once per winter season, however, a caravan new to the route would refuse to hire guides or buy assistance before heading through the Cloud Peaks, only to run into a sudden winter storm or take a wrong turn and require immediate rescue. On occasion, a caravan might only be found again in the next spring.

Almost all the great mercantile families of Athkatla, Crimmor, and Murann maintained an agent or even a family member here to meet caravans venturing north, confirm they had a written manifest for their lord or accountant, and inform the caravanners of news from their destinations.

Enterprising merchants sometimes organized tourist groups in Nashkel, as well as in Baldur's Gate, Beregost, and Berdusk, and took them to visit Durlag's Tower far to the north.

## Population
*Circa* 1357 DR and through 1370 DR, Nashkel had a population of around 4,500 people, with 3,000 based in the town itself and 1,500 in the outer farms and villages. It was majority-human settlement, with 98% humans and 2% halflings, with a smattering of entrepreneurial shield dwarves. The people here were as **Nashkars**.

## Culture
Withstanding the winter storms coming over the Cloud Peaks every year, the locals were hardy and took it in their stride, and would never complain about the weather regardless of how bad it got. Thanks to the risk of the mountain passes being blocked for long periods in winter, the people of Nashkel had a reputation for self-sufficiency and autonomy, at least in day-to-day affairs. Nevertheless, they still thought of themselves as fully Amnian.

## History
Company of the Balefire Banners once suffered a terrible defeat to a vampire in a ruin somewhere north of Nashkel sometime in the 14 century DR.

A country villa belonging to the Tharthyn family stood northwest of Nashkel, before it was abandoned. The Sorlyn priest Velmos Sonder turned into a temple dedicated to Milil, the Abbey of Highsong. Although the abbey prospered, Sonder was murdered there three years after opening, sometime around 1360 DR.

As of the late 1360s DR, travelogue writer Volothamp Geddarm had not yet made it to Nashkel, and not because he was fearful of kobolds or other evil in the mountains. He related what little he knew in his *Volo's Guide to Baldur's Gate*, remarking, "Rumor is that it's a nice place." He promised to try to make it there next time. He would make it there in 1368 DR, staying at the Belching Dragon Tavern.

Sometime before Mirtul in the , the Cyricist cleric Mulahey and his kobold minions began to contaminate the iron ore produced by the mines outside of Nashkel. He did so under the direction of the bandit leader Tazok, and by extension, Rieltar Anchev of the Iron Throne, which schemed to start a war. These events directly led to the Iron Crisis that plagued the Sword Coast in the same year. As tensions built up between Amn and Baldur's Gate, Nashkel became a staging point for Amnian soldiers as they prepared for battle against the Flaming Fist. The adventurer Abdel Adrian and his companions came to Nashkel to investigate the Iron Crisis and bandit attacks in the region, and were recruited by Mayor Berrun Ghastkill to track down missing miners. Whilst exploring the mines, they discovered Mulahey and the kobolds' sabotage and ended their plot, and found evidence leading them to Mulahey's masters. During their time in Nashkel, they were targeted by would-be assassins and bounty hunters. The Rashemi berserker Minsc was also in town seeking help to rescue his witch Dynaheir, and the Red Wizard Edwin was there to kill her. The Sun Soul monk Rasaad also visited Nashkel. The Iron Throne scheme was ultimately thwarted and in time Amn and Baldur's Gate were distracted by other issues.

That same year, a traveling carnival came to Nashkel and set up just east of town. It featured a number of merchant stalls, gambling tents, and even performers such as the Great Gazib and the Amazing Oopah.

The Order of the Gauntlet had an agent in Nashkel in the 1480s DR.

## Notable Locations
- Shops
- Cloud Peaks Outfitters: Equipment shop for mountain travelers run by Salla Pitontoes, a halfling cleric of Tymora.
- Nashkel Store: A general store.
- Plashdeep Stables: A horse trader on Windfields Road, run by Loaber Plashdeep.

- Temples
- Icedawn's House: A shrine to Auril tended by Jasha Istor, who also served as a guide.
- The Temple of Helm: Dedicated to Helm and overseen by the cleric Nalin.
- The Abbey of Highsong: A temple to Mielikki some distance northwest of town.

- Taverns and Inns
- The Belching Dragon: A fairly ordinary, drinks-only tavern.
- The Nashkel Inn: A simple inn.
- The Northern Light: An inn and tavern permanently lit by magical and mundane lights, owing to owner Ries Kensiddar's fear of the dark.

- Other
- Nashkel carnival, a carnival that set up in 1370 DR.
- Nashkel mines, the expansive iron mines nearby in the mountains.
- A refuge created by Maldiglas Turntower south of the town between the landmarks called the Eggs of the Dragon and the Staring Ranger.
- Windfields Road

## Notable Inhabitants
- Mayor Berrun Ghastkill, of the Ghastkill family.
- Daer'Ragh, an anti-social mage.
- Loaber Plashdeep, a horse trader and Harper agent.
- Noober, a very annoying man.
