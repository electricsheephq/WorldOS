# Waendel's Wharf

**Waendel's Wharf** was one of the wharves located in Lower City of Baldur's Gate.

## Location
Waendel's Wharf was the southern end of The Eastern Wet, south of Athcaulyr's Stand. Waendel's Wharf was a thin dock with three legs that protruded from the border of the Brampton district into the Gray Harbor. A small fountain plaza – Sailor's Crossing stood at Waendel's Wharf's northern end and Admiral's Way began on its southern side.
