# Trident Lane

**Trident Lane** was a dockside street in the Lower City of Baldur's Gate.

## Description
Trident Lane was a short street on the shore southwest of the Seatower of Balduran. Further southwest from it was the Silver Crescent Beach and the street's northern side ended at the The Western Wet. The Fury of the Fist stature stood on Trident Lane and the lane itself was home to many businesses and weaponsmiths who serviced the Flaming Fist and its families who resided in the district. One such shop was run by a sentient automaton in the late 15 century DR.

## History
In the summer of the , *circa* Kythorn 19, the city of Baldur's Gate was shuddered by a series of grizzly murders perpetrated by the dragonborn only known as the Dark Urge. One of the victims left being a distinct-looking sacrificial dagger. The adventuring detective named Tav visited the construct smith of Trident Lane on Kythorn 30, seeking the information on the possible murder weapon. The automaton claimed it didn't recognize the blade but a certain githyanki gravedigger attempted to custom-order such a blade. The smith pointed Tav towards a mages' shop on the other side of the city to where it sent the gravedigger.
