# Athcaulyr's Stand

**Athcaulyr's Stand** was one of the wharves located in Lower City of Baldur's Gate.

## Location
Athcaulyr's Stand was the largest legless arrow-shaped wharf in Baldur's Gate. It protruded from the Eastway district into the Gray Harbor. The Eastern Wet street ran across the eastern side of the Gray Harbor and wrapped itself around the entire length of Athcaulyr's Stand. Directly at its southern end was a smaller in size Fisher's Wharf just by the Harbormaster's Office.
