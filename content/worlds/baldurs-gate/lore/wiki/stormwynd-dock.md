# Stormwynd Dock

**Stormwynd Dock** was one of the wharves located in the Lower City of Baldur's Gate.

## Location
Stormwynd Dock and its four legs could be found just south from the Stormshore Street Dock in the Brampton district. It was the closest major dock to the Cliffgate.
