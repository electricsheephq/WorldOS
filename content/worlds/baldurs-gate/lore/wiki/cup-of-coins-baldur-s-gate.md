# Cup of Coins (Baldur's Gate)

**Cup of Coins** was a manycoins services establishment in the city of Baldur's Gate in the 14 century DR.

## Location
The Cup of Coins was located along Bresind Street.

## Activities
In addition to being a moneylending shop, this establishment served as a safehouse for Harpers.

## Inhabitants
The proprietor of this business was Delthyr Iogh.
