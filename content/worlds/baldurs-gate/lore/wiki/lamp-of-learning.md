# Lamp of Learning

The **Lamp of Learning** was a magical academy that operated in Baldur's Gate during the late 15 century DR. It was located in the walled-off district of Little Calimshan, in the Gate's impoverished Outer City.

The school offered training for arcane spellcasters, in the tradition of Calimshan, a nation in the Lands of Intrigue. They only admitted students of Calishite heritage or outsiders of exceptional skill and talent.
