# Whitkeep Hostel

The **Whitkeep Hostel** was a large manor house found in Baldur's Gate that served a number of purposes throughout its existence. Throughout the years it was a private residence, a squatter's pad, and, during the later 15 century DR, a gnomish artists' commune.

It became a gathering place for social progressives and creative-minded folks of all races.

## Description
The hostel was quite spacious, with 37 separate single-room apartments.

## History
The manor originally belonged to a rich caravan master who lost their riches following a number of devastating monster attacks. After their fall from wealth, the building was taken over by a group of squatters. 

By the early 15 century, the hostel began to attract people similar to its current residents.

## Notable Inhabitants
- Pernilla "Prole" Cabrenrock: A vociferous critic of the Baldurian government
- Ardryn Deagle: A rather individualistic mage and inventor
