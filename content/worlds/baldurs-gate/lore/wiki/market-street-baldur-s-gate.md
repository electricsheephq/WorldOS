# Market Street (Baldur's Gate)

**Market Street** was a short street located in the Eastway district of Baldur's Gate, on the border with the Brampton neighborhood.

## Location
Market Street cut through a city block, east to west. The city block was sandwitched between Nuthkhal's Way to the west and Stormshore Street to the east.

## Hisotry
In the , a suspicious half-orc butcher named Magnus Shank was said to had been selling monster meat to the customers. This accusation came at the time of a slew of murders in the Gate, and the butchery was thought to be involved and hide a more sinister secret than monster meat. The half-orc's supplier was a tiefling Selwyn Leventide.
