# Trade Way

The **Trade Way** was a long, well traveled trade road along the west coast of Faerûn. It was classed among the central trade arteries of the continent, being crucial to the economic lifeblood of some nations and city-states. Members of the Lords' Alliance each made contributions to maintain and protect it.

## Geography
The Trade Way spanned from Calimport in the south and along the coast line, known as the Sword Coast, all the way through the Lands of Intrigue and perpendicular to the Starspire Peninsula, until ending in the north at Waterdeep. The section of road between Baldur's Gate and Amn was also known as the Coast Way on some maps, although some maps used this name all the way up to Waterdeep. The section of road that crossed through Amn's northern border, between the cities of Crimmor and Nashkel, was called the Bitter Road. And the thoroughfare between Crimmor and Purskul was known as the Orc Road.

## Geographical features
- Calim Desert: The final leg of the Trade Way in the south. There it stretched between the cities of Calimport to Memnon, with several oases along the way that helped to mark the road. For traders heading north, it was considered an early test of a caravan's strength, whilst for traders coming in from the north it was considered the final challenge of a long and wearisome journey. Those without the will to brave the desert would skirt it by taking the Coast Trail. Additionally, sticking to the Trade Way was considered essential for not getting lost within the desert. In Calimshan's distant past, this stretch of the road was once fully paved stone, capable of supporting three carts or six horses traveling abreast. During the Shoon Imperium, some basic magical protections and tricks of engineering were built into this stone road, ensuring its protection from both Memnon's Crackle and threats beneath, such as purple worms. Over a millennia this road was broken by erosion and sinkholes or buried beneath the sands of the desert, though many of its Shoonite protections survived.
- The Fangs: A pair of steep crags in the Cloud Peaks that the Trade Way directly passed through, marking the boundaries between Amn and the Sword Coast.
- Forest of Tethir: A major forest in Tethyr that the Trade Way directly cut through. The areas of the forest within  of the Trade Way were particularly cultivated by the Elmanesse to be very dense in their foliage, ensuring it would be difficult for outsiders or penetrate or investigate.
- Misty Forest: A forest astride the Trade Way in Delimbiyr Vale.  It was frequently utilized by human barbarians and orcs to sneak up on the Trade Way and the Way Inn. However, their surprise attacks were usually spoiled by forewarnings from human foresters and wild elves.
- Waypost Water : A small pond about half a days ride north of Daggerford.

### Landmarks
- Minarets of the Calim Desert: Another surviving relic of the Shoon Imperium were pairs of slim,  tall minaret towers, that flanked the Trade Way every  in the Calim Desert. Housed within the open rooftop parapets of their lookout posts were brass braziers. When a pair of towers had their braziers lit, a crystal inset at the top of their pointed roofs heated up and cloaked the road in a dazzling, translucent blue field of magic. These magical fields extended , up and down the road, from the towers' position. These fields kept sand off the stone road, blocked much of the bright daytime glare given off by the desert sands and salt flats, and halved the force of any wind traveling over the road from up to . During the Imperium's heyday, these towers would have been constantly lit. The later Syl-Pasha Ralan el Pesarkhal sought to implement a reconstruction effort, feeling that the minarets would bring a new sense of safety to Calimshan's section of the Trade Way, half travel times across his nation, and usher in a trade boom. By 1370 DR, work would be completed on at least the two towers closest to Calimport.

### Settlements
- Baldur's Gate : A major port city astride the Trade Way. The majority of its Outer City ran along the section known as "Coast Way". From the north it passed through the city's Blackgate district, which serviced those passing to and from Waterdeep. There traders were forced to stable their pack animals while their goods were transferred to another conveyance while traveling through the city. From the south, the Trade Way passed over Wyrm's Crossing and into the district of Rivington. 
- Boareskyr Bridge : A combination bridge and settlement that spanned the Winding Water. Between the settlements of Scornubel and Waterdeep, it was the last "organized" trade post one could find until reaching the Way Inn.
- Bowshot : A hamlet that was half a days ride north of the Way Inn.
- Calimport : Located at the southern end of the great Trade Way, it directly entered this city through a sector known as Trades Ward.
- Daggerford : North of Bowshot. The largest stop on the Trade Way between Waterdeep and Soubar. It was situated where the Trade Way crossed the Delimbiyr River. Its farmlands were considered to be among the the calmest and safest small stretches in the Trade Way.
- Gillian's Hill : Located about a half-day's travel south of Daggerford.
- Imnescar: A small town in southwest Amn that was a popular way station and overnight stop for those traveling along the Trade Way. There it connected with Imnescourse Trail. 
- Liam's Hold : Located about a day's travel south of Daggerford.
- Manshaka: A coastal town in Calimshan situated near the northern edge of the Calim Desert. Due its location nearly every caravan on the Trade Way stopped there at for at least a full day, either prepare for the ardous journey into the desert or to recuperate from their journey through it.
- Memnon : Another city in Calimshan that was located at the very northern edges of the Calim Desert.
- Mosstone: A caravan stop in Tethyr situated just outside where the Trade Way exited the Forest of Tethir from the southwest. The livelihoods of many Mosstonians relied upon the traffic that the Trade Way brought them.
- Myratma: The second largest city in Tethyr.
- Nashkel: The northernmost city of Amn, situated north of the Cloud Peaks.
- Port Kir: A port city in Tethyr that was south of Mosstone.
- Purskul: An important trade and freight center in western Amn. A great deal of grain arrived to the city through the Trade Way and was often either graded, brokered, or stored there.
- Scornubel : Located where the Trade Way meets the River Chionthar. It was considered one of the key cities on the Trade Way leading from Waterdeep.
- Soubar : A small town situated south of Boareskyr Bridge and north of Triel along the Trade Way.
- Trademeet: A crossroads town in Amn situated at where the Trade Way and Tethir Road intersected. It was the smallest of Amn's caravan stops.
- Triel : A waystation north of Scornubel, situated where the Trade Way met up with the lesser-used Dusk Road.
- Waterdeep : A major port city at the northern end of the Trade Way. It directly entered this city through its Southern Ward, wherein it connected to the city's High Road.
- Wayfork: A village on the Sword Coast astride the Trade Way, just south of Dragonspear Castle.
- Way Inn : A small village on the Sword Coast that the Trade Way ran through the middle of. It was situated half a days ride south of Bowshot.
- Zazesspur: A major port city in Tethyr, a center of commerce and trade in the nation.

## History
The well-traveled route that became known by many as the "Trade Way" coalesced over the centuries from numerous separate caravan paths that connected a chain of city-states along the Sword Coast. In the land of Tethyr, the area that would later host the Trade Way in the Principality of the Corsairs' Sea was initially part of the Forest of Tethir, but was cleared down around the -80's DR by Clan Karlag.
