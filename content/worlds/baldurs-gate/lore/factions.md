# Factions of Baldur's Gate

*Era: 1492 DR, after the Absolute.*

**The Flaming Fist** — the mercenary company founded by Eltan that serves as the Gate's standing army and police. Led by **Marshal Ulder Ravengard** (father of the hero Wyll), lately freed from the Absolute's grip and stretched thin. With the Steel Watch gone, the Fist is the only law, and it is exhausted and political at the top.

**The Guild** — Baldur's Gate's vast thieves' network: smuggling, protection, and information, run for years by **Nine-Fingers Keene** from the Counting House beneath the Lower City. Pragmatic and everywhere; often the only ones who know what truly moves in the dark.

**The Zhentarim (Black Network)** — a Bane-tinged mercantile-mercenary cabal expanding into the power vacuum with coin, contracts, and quiet muscle. Smiling and ruthless.

**The Harpers** — the covert network that fights tyranny and unchecked power, battered by the Absolute war but watching the contested dukedom closely. Idealists with knives; the veteran **Jaheira** is a High Harper.

**The patriars** — the noble families (Ravengard and others) who rule the Upper City through the Parliament of Peers and maneuver, now, for the empty Archduke's seat.

**The Gur** — wanderers and monster-hunters camped in Rivington and the Outer City; some hunt the vampire spawn loosed when Cazador fell.

**Remnants of the Absolute** — scattered cultists, abandoned "true souls," Bhaalist murder-cults, and freed illithids who still hear an echo of the brain. Broken, desperate, and dangerous.
