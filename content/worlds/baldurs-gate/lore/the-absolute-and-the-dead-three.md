# The Absolute Crisis & the Dead Three

*Era: just ended, 1492 DR. This is recent history — its wreckage is everywhere.*

**The Absolute Crisis.** The Cult of the Absolute used the **Crown of Karsus** to enslave an Elder Brain — the **Netherbrain** — and spread illithid tadpoles across the Sword Coast, building toward mass ceremorphosis. It was served by three **Chosen** of the Dead Three:
- **Ketheric Thorm**, Chosen of Shar, who raised the Shadow Curse over Reithwin and Moonrise Towers and imprisoned the Nightsong.
- **Enver Gortash**, Chosen of Bane, who seized the Archduke's seat through the Steel Watch.
- **Orin the Red**, Chosen of Bhaal, a shapeshifting murderer.

All three were destroyed, and the Netherbrain with them, at the **Battle of Baldur's Gate**. The **Emperor** — a mind flayer that helped end the crisis and serves only itself — was, in secret, once **Balduran** himself.

**The Dead Three.** Three dark gods whose cults persist past their Chosen:
- **Bane**, god of tyranny and ambition — Banite plots and the Zhentarim's edge.
- **Bhaal**, god of murder — Bhaalist murder-shrines still fester in the Gate's underbelly.
- **Myrkul**, god of the dead.

**Aftermath.** Tadpole-survivors are feared; the dukedom is empty; the Steel Watch foundry under Wyrm's Rock is wrecked; and loose ends — cultists, a stray illithid, abandoned vampire spawn — did not all crawl home.
