WorldOS — GT2 Pillars/BG backdrop-isometric (M2)

Tier-2 renderer: a Pillars/Baldur's-Gate-style painted backdrop with depth-sorted token actors, driven by the WorldOS surfaces. Reads /atlas-surface + /character-surface + /combat-surface + /events; writes only intents to /move — click the walkable floor to move (→ {kind:move_to_zone}) or an exit (→ {kind:travel}). The walkmask is renderer-owned presentation (the engine knows location adjacency, not walkable pixels); destinations still resolve to engine zones/locations. Combat renders as a pure replay of engine combat (paused/turn, no client dice). Backdrops + tokens are procedural placeholders until the #447 painted-backdrop asset pipeline lands; real art then swaps in via the render-profile scope keys with no scene change. Flat-lit MVP (normal-map lighting is M5/Branch B). Standalone on bundled fixtures; add ?campaign=<id> (optional ?base=…) for a live game.

Served at /openworlds/render/backdrop.html — self-contained sub-app, zero edits to the shared viewer. See README.md.
