WorldOS — GT1 SNES-pixel tilemap (M1)

Tier-1 renderer: a 16-bit top-down tilemap driven by the WorldOS surfaces. Reads /atlas-surface + /character-surface + /combat-surface + /events; writes only intents to /move (tap a green exit tile → {kind:travel}). Combat renders as zone bands (pure replay of engine combat — no client dice, no VTT grid). Tiles + tokens are procedural placeholders until the #439 pixel-asset pipeline lands (the owner's image-model decision); real art then swaps in via the render-profile scope keys with no scene change. Standalone on bundled fixtures; add ?campaign=<id> (optional ?base=…) for a live game.

Served at /openworlds/render/tilemap.html — self-contained sub-app, zero edits to the shared viewer. See README.md.
